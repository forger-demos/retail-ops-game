Settings (Game Boy menu)

Opened with S from the title. Arrow keys move the cursor, left/right change a value, ENTER on BACK returns. Values persist in the browser.

DAY

Shift hours (8). Speed: 1 s = 5 min. Staff on floor (1-4). Customers per hour (10). Mix orders per hour (3).

PEOPLE

Service time (4 min). Patience (10 min). Average sale GBP (95).

MIXING

Minutes per tin (5). Order size mix: 1 tin / 2-3 / 4-5 / 6+ percentages. Tin prices 750ml / 2.5L / 5L. Hub threshold: over 5 tins.

SCALE

Stores (N), trading days per year.

RESHUFFLE DAY RESET DEFAULTS BACK

Empty

First visit shows the defaults.

Failed

Browser storage unavailable: settings still apply for this session; a one-line note says they will not be remembered.

Full

Order-size percentages that do not total 100 show "MUST TOTAL 100" and BACK is blocked until fixed.
