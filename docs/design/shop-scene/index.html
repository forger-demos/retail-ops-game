Shop scene (autoplay)

One fixed 160x144 Game Boy screen, scaled up by whole pixels and centred; letterboxed on wide or tall displays. Top-down view.

Status bar

Scenario name ("TODAY" or "WITH CENTRAL HUB") - shop clock 09:00 to 17:00 - served / lost counters - lost GBP so far.

Shop floor

Door, aisles, counter. Customers walk in, queue at the counter, show a wait meter. At the patience limit an "!" bubble appears and they walk out; a "-GBP95" pops.

Mixing room

Behind a wall. Staff who take a mix order walk here; a tin-progress bar fills per tin ("TIN 3/8"). The floor visibly empties.

Loading bay (fix run only)

Orders over 5 tins are sent off: a van drives away with "2H DELIVERY".

Text box

Pokemon-style dialogue box at the bottom for narration beats: "A customer wants 8 tins mixed!", "Nobody on the floor...", "Customer left!". Autoplay advances on its own; SPACE skips ahead.

Empty

Shop opens at 09:00 with no customers; staff stand at the counter.

Loading

None needed - assets are inlined; the first frame is the shop at opening.

Failed

Canvas unsupported: plain text "This demo needs a modern browser" plus the results as text.

Full

Queue at the counter is capped visually at its slots; extra waiting customers stand by the door, still counted.
