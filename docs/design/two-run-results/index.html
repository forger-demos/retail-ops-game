Flow: title, today, results, fix, comparison

SPACE advances every step. All screens share the 160x144 frame.

1. Title

"PAINT RUSH" logo, a tin sprite, "PRESS SPACE". Small hint: "S - settings, M - sound".

2. Run: TODAY

The shop scene, all mixing done in store.

3. Results: TODAY

Text box list: tins mixed, customers served, customers lost, sales lost GBP.

4. Run: WITH CENTRAL HUB

Same seed, same customers and orders. Orders over 5 tins go to the van.

5. Comparison

Two columns, TODAY vs HUB: customers lost, sales lost. Then "SAVED PER DAY", per week, per year, across N stores. Footnote: "Excludes central prep and delivery costs."

SPACE - replay / R - reshuffle day

Empty

A day with no lost customers still shows the table, with zeros and "No sales lost today".

Loading

None.

Failed

None - results are computed, not fetched.

Full

Large GBP values are shortened ("GBP1.2M") to fit the 20-column text box.

The results are also written to a visually hidden live region, so a screen reader and the a11y check can read them.
