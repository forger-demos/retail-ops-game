# Glossary

- **Walk-in** - a customer on the shop floor who wants to buy and needs a staff member to serve them.
- **Lost sale** - a walk-in who waits past their patience and leaves without buying; valued at its customer type's value.
- **Patience** - how long a walk-in waits for a free staff member before leaving.
- **Service time** - how long serving one walk-in occupies a staff member.
- **Staff on the floor** - staff available to serve; a staff member mixing is off the floor.
- **Mix order** - a request for paint tinted to order, of one or more tins.
- **Tin** - one container of mixed paint, sized 750ml, 2.5L or 5L. Mixing takes minutes-per-tin regardless of size.
- **Large order** - a mix order with more tins than the hub threshold (over 5 by default).
- **Hub** - the central site that prepares large orders and delivers them to the customer within 2 hours.
- **Scenario** - one way of running the shop: `today` (all mixing in store) or `hub` (large orders to the hub).
- **Shift** - one trading day, simulated in shop minutes.
- **Time scale** - real seconds per shop minute; by default 1 s stands for 5 shop minutes, one tin.
- **Seed** - the random seed that fixes arrivals; both scenarios of a run share it.
- **Timeline** - the ordered events of a simulated shift, replayed by the renderer.
- **Replay** - the shop scene playing one scenario's timeline at the time scale.
- **Beat** - one line of narration in the text box, derived from the timeline.
- **Comparison** - the last step of the pitch: both scenarios' lost walk-ins and sales side by side, and the sales saved.
- **Sales saved** - today's sales lost minus the hub's, for one shift; scaled by trading days per year to a year, by a 52nd of that to a week, and by the number of stores to the estate. It excludes the hub's prep and delivery costs.
- **Round** - one short played shift in play mode; round 1 mixes everything in store, round 2 sends large orders to the hub. It runs at its own, faster time scale and opens on the same scripted trade order in both rounds.
- **Press** - one player action in a round (serve, mix, send to hub) at a shop minute; a round's presses are its decision policy, and replaying them gives the same round.
- **Time unmanned** - shop minutes in a round with nobody on the floor.
- **Play mode** - the default experience: the audience is the shop assistant and chooses every action.
- **Presenter mode** - the v1 autoplay flow, for narrating without playing.
- **Debrief** - after the rounds: the round comparison, then the whole real day, the full-shift simulation of today vs hub on autoplay, and the extrapolation.
- **Round comparison** - the two rounds side by side, as played, and which made more in sales.
- **Style frame** - one finished frame of the real look, approved by the owner before the screens are built on it.
- **Juice** - feedback for a player action: something moves, something sounds, something changes on screen.
- **Customer type** - what a walk-in wants: colour advice, a sample pot or a small purchase (a mix order is its own thing). Each has a share of walk-ins, a value if served, a look and a bubble. Shares total 100 and the values, weighted by share, average to the average sale.
